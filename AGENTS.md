# AGENTS.md

## Source map

- `index.html`: page shell, map overlays, controls, chapter strip, tooltip, sources drawer.
- `src/story.ts`: chapter content: steps (narrative, cameras, map marks, insets, quotes), region and sea names, war phases, the alliance timeline.
- `src/places.ts`: site coordinates `[lon, lat]`, checked against Pleiades.
- `src/main.ts`: renderer, step controller (navigation, autoplay, exploring), insets and diagrams, place tooltips.
- `src/placeNotes.ts`: one- or two-sentence notes per place for the map tooltip, with citations.
- `src/style.css`: tokens (light and dark), map, cards, insets.
- `src/data/land.json`: baked coastline, generated by `scripts/build-land.mjs`; never edit by hand.
- `public/media/`: openly licensed artifact photos; `credits.json` holds attribution for each file.
- `docs/research.md`: verified source brief behind the narrative.
- `scripts/`: coastline build, frame capture, autoplay recording, deterministic video export.

## Engineering invariants

- Keep the site static. No server, accounts, or runtime APIs.
- The story is a sequence of steps. Every frame is a pure function of the current step, the seconds since it was entered, and an ambient clock used only for small idle motion. Navigation (wheel, keys, swipes, buttons, the chapter strip), autoplay, and frame capture (`?step=12&t=4`) all just choose a step and a time; this is what makes video export possible.
- The reader can always explore: dragging or zooming detaches only the camera ("回到故事" reattaches it), and hovering or tapping a place opens its tooltip. Exploring never changes the step.
- Autoplay is documentary mode: the card gives way to narration written onto the map, the map takes the whole screen, and a chapter tag introduces each step. Reading mode (manual or paused) shows the card with its citations.
- Nothing covers anything (`claims`, `arrange`). Renderers do not decide visibility against each other; they register what they draw, and one pass at the end of the frame settles it in priority order: chrome, inset, callout and narration; city dots and tour pins (fixed in place, fading beneath those); city names (eight positions around the dot, sticky, a wider ring when a pin sits on the city); ships' tags; region and sea names. What finds no room fades, quickly out and gently back.
- Narration lives where the eye is (`renderNarration`, `placeCaption`, `captionObstacles`). Each sentence is written on beside the places, regions or seas it names (Athens and Sparta anchor only when nothing else is named), else near the previous sentence. It goes in the clearest nearby spot: not over the step's cities, labels, routes (including the part not drawn yet), pins, inset or chrome. Captions are stored in world units and ride the map. Never put narration back in a fixed bottom bar.
- The map acts when the narration names it (`cuesAt`, `fractionAt`, `animationTime`): in documentary mode each visual plays across the sentence that names it (a route's stops, a pulse's place, a city changing hands), or else the sentence at its point in the step. Writing a name into the body is what times its visual.
- Pacing is arithmetic, never hand-timed (`READING_RATE`, `WORTH`, `subtitlePlan`, `visualsOf`, `insetCost`, `markCost`, `beatHold`, `interludeCost`). Everything a viewer takes in is measured in characters of reading: text by length, other information through the exchange rates in `WORTH` (a glance, a figure, an image, route pixels, refocusing across the screen, savouring a quotation). Insets are measured from their own rendering. Each sentence holds for its text, the look it takes to find it, and the visuals it brings in; cities changing hands together cost one pattern (log of their count). Seconds = characters ÷ `READING_RATE` ÷ speed. Change the pace with `READING_RATE`, the density of a kind of information with `WORTH`; do not add per-step or per-element durations. Only camera flights are physics (`flightMs`). Check with `window.__atlas.timing()` and `window.__atlas.budget()` after content changes. One reading task at a time: the tour flies to a beat only after the narration, and autoplay tours only the first beat of a step (`TOUR_BEATS`); it ends there and the next step flies on from the beat.
- Attention is directed, not left to chance (`focusAt`, `routeStops`, `arrivalAt`, `spotlightAt`). In a step with a subject, cities outside it all but vanish (`RECEDE`; Athens and Sparta stay faint as bearings) and region/sea names step back. Cities a route passes stay hidden until its head reaches them, then light up with a swell, ripple and label. While a route draws or a beat is toured, the base map darkens slightly away from that point. New routes need no extra wiring: named `via` places and subject cities near the line become stops automatically.
- Keep the camera stage free of the card column on desktop and of the card area on mobile.
- The base map (sea, coastline, waterlines, shadow, influence glows, grain) is painted on one canvas and only redrawn when the camera or glows change; SVG holds the light, interactive layers. Do not move large paths back into SVG or put blend modes or backdrop filters over the moving map.
- Story time comes from `clock()`. Camera moves use the quintic `glide` ease; autoplay flights are longer, and the camera drifts in slowly while it rests and releases the drift on the next flight, so motion never stops or jumps.
- Light is the default look and does not follow the OS. Dark is an opt-in look (`?theme=dark`) for particular uses such as exports; keep both palettes working.
- Do not commit `dist/`, `artifacts/`, or verification output.

## Content invariants

- Every factual sentence that a reader could dispute carries a citation to a primary source by book.chapter.section (Thucydides, Xenophon, Herodotus, Plutarch, Pseudo-Xenophon), checked against a translation. Later sources (Plutarch, Diodorus) are labelled as such.
- Quotations are paraphrased in Chinese from English translations; never present them as a published Chinese translation.
- Mark modern estimates as estimates and keep them out of quotation marks. Respect the pitfalls listed in `docs/research.md` (for example, 5,000 : 35,000 is the army at Plataea in 479, not Sparta's population).
- Write the Chinese natively; standard transliterations (修昔底德, 伯里克利, 亚西比德, 吕山德, 布拉西达斯, 尼西阿斯).
- Images: only public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA, each with an entry in `public/media/credits.json` and a visible credit where it is shown.
- Schematic diagrams say they are schematic. Hand-drawn geography (Sphacteria) is disclosed in the footer.

## Task routing

- Narrative or map change: `src/story.ts`; check facts against `docs/research.md` first and extend it when adding beats.
- New place: `src/places.ts` with Pleiades-checked coordinates, plus a note in `src/placeNotes.ts`.
- New mark or inset type: add the type in `src/story.ts`, the renderer in `src/main.ts`, styles in `src/style.css`.
- Coastline change: `scripts/build-land.mjs`, then `npm run build:land`.

## Verification

```sh
npm run check                     # type-check and build
npm run preview -- --port 4500 &  # serve dist/
node scripts/capture-frames.mjs artifacts/frames 1440 900 light 1:7,4:7,8:7
node scripts/capture-frames.mjs artifacts/frames-mobile 390 844 dark 1:7,4:7,8:7
node scripts/record-autoplay.mjs artifacts/video
node scripts/export-video.mjs artifacts/export 1 3   # frame-exact video of steps 1–3 + camera.json
npm run audit:overlaps            # whole film, desktop: must report zero overlaps
npm run audit:overlaps -- 1 33 2 390 844   # and on a phone
```

Real-time recordings stutter when the machine is busy; judge motion with `export-video.mjs`, which renders each frame on a virtual clock, and check `camera.json` for jumps in camera velocity.

`audit-overlaps.mjs` plays the documentary on the virtual clock and reports every pair of visible elements whose boxes overlap where they must not (names, dots, pins, tags, region names, narration, callout, inset, chrome). Fix overlaps through the arrangement pass, not by nudging one element.

Look at the frames and at a contact sheet of the recording (desktop and mobile, light and dark) before publishing. A clean build does not prove layout, motion, or legibility. The autoplay recording must finish with zero console errors.
